Privacy

Privacy policy for &games. Last updated 22 November 2026.

This covers all the &games apps: Paddle, Bowling, Pump, Arcade, Boards, Cards, Dice, Puzzles, Cue Sports, Flick Sports, Quiz, Kids and Sikh Heritage. In plain words: the games don't collect anything about you.

What the games keep

Only what makes the game work, and only on your own phone, iPad or Mac:

  • Your scores, best times and progress.
  • Player names you type in (for example "Me" or "Grandad"), so scores go to the right person.
  • Your settings, such as level, sound and colours, and any game you leave part-way through.

None of this is sent anywhere. Deleting a game deletes it all.

Playing with someone nearby

Some games let two phones play together on the same Wi-Fi. The phones talk directly to each other and send only game moves. Nothing goes through a server, and nothing is stored anywhere else. Your phone asks your permission the first time.

Motion and the torch

A few games (such as Pump, and Bowling in its own app or inside Flick Sports) can use your phone's motion sensors to read a swing or a throw. Pump can also blink your phone's torch on each shot, if you switch that on, which needs camera permission. Neither the motion readings nor the camera are recorded, stored or shared, and no pictures are taken.

What the games do not do

  • No adverts and no tracking.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or third-party tools.
  • No accounts, passwords or sign-in.
  • No purchases inside the games.
  • Nothing about you is sold or given to anyone.

Children

The games are made for families, including children. They don't ask anyone for personal details, and the player names stay on the device.

This website

andgames.ink counts how many times its pages are viewed, using GoatCounter. It sets no cookies and keeps nothing that identifies you: just the page, the date, the kind of browser and screen, the country, and which site linked here. The games themselves never use it.
